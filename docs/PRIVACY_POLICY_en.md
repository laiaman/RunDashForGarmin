# RunDash Privacy Policy (draft — host as a public URL before store submit)

Last updated: 2026-10-01

## Scope

This policy applies to the Connect IQ Data Field **RunDash** (“the App”).

## What we access

The App runs locally on your Garmin watch during activities (e.g. running).

- **On-device metrics**: heart rate, cadence, time, distance, pace, battery, and similar activity values provided by the Garmin device.
- **User Profile permission**: when granted, the App reads heart-rate zone settings from the watch user profile to draw the zone bar.
- **App settings**: values you enter (target pace, fallback max HR, virtual pacer toggle) are stored in Connect IQ app settings on device / companion flow.
- **The App does not create an account and does not upload, sell, or transmit your workout or personal data to the developer’s servers.**

## How data is used

Data is used only to render and compute the on-watch display. It is not used for advertising profiles or third-party analytics by this App.

## Third parties

Distribution uses the Garmin Connect IQ platform. Garmin’s own policies apply to your Garmin account, downloads, and device sync.
